manga
our dining table
★★★★★

ok so i got the first volume...of the sequel to this series! so i just had to go back and read the actual series. or standalone. and boy did it not dissapoint! it felt very domestic: the way they cooked together and ate together. the feeling of safety and love towards those you spend your time around. although it was short, it was nothing less than sweet. minoru falling in love with yutaka 2 years in advance beacuse of the kindness he showed to tane...nothing short of peak. like wow. i wasn't expecting that but i'm so happy. i love it so much and i REALLY want to buy a physical ♡

manga
boukansha no koi
★★★★☆

stressed me out but noah grew on me in the end. it was very sad and i read it in the middle of the night so i was just sobbing. it was bad idea. it was very obvious that the girl he had fallen in love with WAS rachel but like it's ok i'd be that dense too ♡

manga
i'm here beside you vol 1 + 2
★★★★☆

doi was a bit of a crybaby but honestly that made him kind of endearing to me! i was super taken aback by baba's uh...disgust? when doi confessed. like i audibly gasped. i don't know what i was expecting though. doi was the main reason i liked this. i feel like the romance was a bit rushed but it was still enjoyable! i wish the series was longer ♡

manga
fall in love false angels vol 1
★★★★★

ok. to be completely honest i had some preconcieved ideas about this manga, mostly including that it was gonna be trash because the male lead cut his hair. after reading it, here is my stance. even if he cuts his hair it'll be okay because i really like the dynamic between ninomae and katsura! they're both able to be themselves around each other and i think that's lovely. also the art is so beautiful, and light ninomae with his hair down is just gorgeous. genuinely gorgeous. i'm not sure if the library has the other volumes, but if they don't i'm definitely going to continue reading this online! stay tuned ♡

anime
puella magi madoka magica
★★★★★

i got recommended this show from a friend of mine, and at first i was very skeptical. but now, i've realized that i've been sleeping on peak! this show is so amazing, and even though i kind of already knew the plot, i was still left in complete awe. i watched the movie as well and continued to be speechless. the love between madoka and homura truly transcends this universe. i can't wait to watch the movie this august ♡
